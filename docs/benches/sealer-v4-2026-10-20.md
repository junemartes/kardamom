# Sealer load bench: decision version 4 (2026-10-20)

Decision version 4 against the baseline in `sealer-v3-2026-10-20.md`. Both use the same host and the same engine settings. The setup is the same as in the baseline:
- one host: 12 cores, a load average of 12 to 22 from other work;
- two twins that offer the same records in batches of 64, and one consumer.

The engine settings are: blocks of 250 ms, a horizon of 8 blocks, a window of 4,194,304 ids, and `-Xmx2g -XX:+UseSerialGC`.

- **The v4 runs.** The bench switches the cluster to version 4 before the run (`-Pbench.v4Ids=true`): the activation record of feature 3, then its epoch. The twins then put the stream position of each reference in its deadline field.
- **The batcher stand-in.** The consumer posts a cursor 4 blocks behind each boundary, as a batcher does, so the members evict their retained frames.

## What changed

- **The state.** A reference with its position takes the replay window of its ingress image. The window is fixed arrays, so the record path of the state allocates nothing (`AllocationGateTest`, case `ref_v4`).
- **The egress.** The relay copies the payload from the log buffer into a byte ring of retained frames, and offers it to each session from one staging buffer. The session list is an array that changes only when a session opens, closes or subscribes. A frame is copied to an array only when a session is back-pressured.
- **The profile.** At the maximum rate on one member, the sampled allocation fell from 2,098 MB to 220 MB. Most of the rest is the growth of the retained ring to its steady size in the warm-up.

## What limits the rate now

- **No GC.** No member paused for GC in any run.
- **The driver thread.** The busiest thread of a member is the media driver (`SHARED` threading with the capped idle strategy of the deploy), not the service.
- **The shared host.** In the 3-member runs, three members, the twins and the consumer share 12 busy cores. On the deploy, each member has its own server.

The next steps are to run the bench on the sealer hosts, and to try `DEDICATED` driver threading for the hot path.
