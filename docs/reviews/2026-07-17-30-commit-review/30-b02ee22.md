# 30 b02ee22 — refactor(deployer): remove dead/legacy code and tech debt (#50)

## Summary of change
Small dead-code sweep of `kardamom-deployer`: removes the zero-caller `addresses::app_proxy_address` offline CREATE2 derivation helper (the deployer read app proxy addresses from the factory's on-chain registry instead) and three unused dependencies (`serde` — only `serde_json` is used; `tracing` — only `tracing-subscriber` is used; `url` — `rpc_url.parse()` targets alloy's `Url`). Cargo.lock updated accordingly.

## Findings

Dead-code verification: at the b02ee22 tree, `app_proxy_address` has zero references anywhere (its helper `proxy_full_initcode` remains legitimately used by deployer.rs:176 and `factory_proxy_address`), and no `use serde`/`tracing::`/`url::` occurrences exist in deployer src/bins/tests, so all three dependency removals are correct. The conservative keeps (`alloy-*` for `sol!` plumbing, `ContractId::proxy_salt`/`ALL`, `Op` accessors, the print-factory-address bin) all still exist and are referenced at HEAD. No tests, error handling, or metrics were removed — `app_proxy_address` had no tests of its own.

### F30.1 [low] [quality] — Removed helper was needed again five commits later and was reimplemented
- **Where**: crates/deployer/src/addresses.rs (removed at b02ee22); reintroduced at crates/deployer/src/addresses.rs:83 and used at crates/deployer/src/deployer.rs:150 at HEAD
- **What**: `app_proxy_address` was genuinely dead at this commit, but the withdrawals feature (637f683, #71) reintroduced an equivalent function with the same name and signature — this time documented (predicting a proxy address to wire into another contract's init data within an atomic batch) and using `create2_from_code` rather than `create2(salt, keccak256(full))`. The removal was correct by the stated dead-code policy, but the "abandoned offline derivation" judgment was wrong in hindsight: the capability was a planned need, and deleting it created re-implementation churn. No dangling reference existed at any point.
- **Still present at HEAD**: no (superseded by the reimplementation in 637f683; nothing to fix)
- **Suggested fix**: None needed now. Process note: for pure, side-effect-free address-derivation primitives that mirror on-chain factory behavior, prefer keeping (with a test) or checking roadmap intent before removal.

## Verdict
Correct removal-only commit by its own rules: the helper and all three dependencies were verifiably unreferenced at the commit, and the keep/remove judgment calls were documented and check out at HEAD. Its only blemish is retrospective — the deleted derivation helper turned out to be a planned capability that the withdrawals work re-added shortly after — which is churn, not breakage. No actionable issues at HEAD.
