# cross-crate

## Summary

The six service binaries are much thinner than the prior audit found. Most of the
prior cross-cutting plan is done: `kardamom_obs::bin` owns tracing and shutdown,
`AeronRuntime::spawn(Option<&Path>)` killed 13 branch copies, `open_auto` owns the
tx_receipts MDS fan-in, and `bin_support` owns the checkpoint ladder, the cluster
connect, and the resync fallback. Four bin-level shapes remain: the
`LogConfig::resolve` + `AeronRuntime::spawn` open block in all six bins, the
`ResumePoint` build in executor and validator, the recorder spawn-and-barrier
wrapper in ingress and da-watcher, and the `--log-config` / `--aeron-dir` clap
pair in all six. Outside the bins, two consensus rules still have two copies each:
`partition_for` (ingress and sequencer) and the `Receipt` field-by-field build
(exec-core and stm). The largest mass is in tests: one `EngineWiring` test double
set is copied in five files across two crates and a bench, the signed-legacy-tx
builder has about 14 copies, `bpos`/`pos` re-implements the existing
`BPosition::from_index` about 12 times, and the five `metrics_endpoint.rs` files
are near-identical. Estimated reduction: about 270 lines of production code and
about 870 lines of test code, so about 1,140 lines in total.
