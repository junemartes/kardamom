# exec-core

## Summary

The group has four implementations of one derived-transaction shape: the free
`execute_deposit_tx` and `execute_xchain_tx`, plus `Executor::execute_deposit`
and `Executor::execute_xchain`. Two of them are live at the same time. The
streaming executor calls the free `execute_xchain_tx`, while the validator, the
whole-block path, and the zk guest call `Executor::execute_xchain`. This is one
consensus rule with two bodies. Collapsing it removes about 380 production
lines and the largest drift risk in the group. Three more consensus rules are
copied instead of shared: the ZERO-versus-KECCAK_EMPTY code-hash mapping (7
sites), the EIP-161 empty-account test (3 sites), and BAL quantization, which
`bal_ladder.rs` writes twice with two different algorithms. The estimated
production reduction is about 720 lines, and the estimated test reduction is
about 460 lines, out of about 10,500 lines in the group. Both prior-audit items
that touch this group are done: `seed_cache_layer` and `execute_record_in_scope`
now exist and every caller uses them.
