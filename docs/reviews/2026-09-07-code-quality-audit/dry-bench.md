# bench

## Summary

The largest shape in this group is the channel-backed engine test wiring. Six files
declare the same `ChanTxDataSub`, `ChanTxOrderingSub`, `ChanReceiptsPub`, `Imm`,
`TestWiring` and `bpos` set, about 280 lines. A `testing` feature on
`kardamom-engine` removes almost all of it. The second shape is the bench
transaction factory: eleven copies of the Anvil mnemonic, six copies of the funded
`MockStateDatabase` builder, and five copies of the sign-and-encode legacy
transaction block. The third is `stm-p0.rs` and `stm-p2.rs`, which share 97 lines of
scenario builders verbatim. Production code gives about 600 removable lines and
tests give about 480 in this group, plus about 270 more in other crates that the same
helpers unlock. Two prior-audit items that touch this group stay open: `resume_point`
is still copied between the executor and the validator, and the bench binaries keep
three private `init_tracing` copies although `kardamom_obs::bin::init_tracing` now
exists. `AeronRuntime::spawn`, `connect_cluster_ordering` and
`replay_unavailable_fallback` are done.
